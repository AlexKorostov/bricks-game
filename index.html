How to Play

Strategic 4-way brick puzzle

1
Launch Bricks: Click any brick on the innermost layer of the Top, Bottom, Left, or Right walls.
2
Form Lines: Bricks slide until hitting an obstacle. Connect 3 or more of the same color in a horizontal or vertical line to clear them.
3
Cascades: Landed bricks retain their flight momentum! When blocking bricks disappear, freed bricks slide again to create massive chain combos.
4
Clear the Field: Empty all bricks from the central 10×10 board to complete the wave!
